# What Makes a Good Programmer
Link: [https://henrikwarne.com/2014/06/30/what-makes-a-good-programmer/](https://henrikwarne.com/2014/06/30/what-makes-a-good-programmer/)

I found this article really interesting for a few different reasons but the main one is that it touched on something that is super important to me and is actually why I am studying computer science. I have been coding since I was 13 but was never particularly amazing at it nor is it something I am obsessed with, even today, I can't visuallize myself coding for 8 hours a day every day. I didn't major in computer science with that in mind but instead what I do love is problem solving. I really enjoy tackling difficult problems and finding elegant solutions. 

This article really resonated with me because it remined me that there are many parts of being a good programmer and many of them do not have to do with how good you are at remembering all the fuctions and capabilities of a language. It talks about skills that programmers need to have that I think make for good programmers in the long run. Things like problem solving, sernario analysis and constantly wanting to learn more are things that I can say that I feel pretty good. I can also see the importance of these skills now that I have been programming for a while and it is nice to be reminded that coding isn't all of it.
